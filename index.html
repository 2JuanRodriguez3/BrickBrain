BrickBrain

Intelligent LEGO Build System

Juan Rodriguez | SIP 409

About Me

Hi, I'm Juan Rodriguez, an Advanced Computer Science student at UAT with a passion for AI, software development, and LEGO building.

Problem

LEGO enthusiasts often have large collections of unused or leftover pieces. Existing tools focus on official sets and instructions, not creative reuse of inventory.

Innovation / Solution

BrickBrain combines artificial intelligence and inventory management with LEGO creativity to help users discover new and unique build possibilities from their existing collections.

Design Process

Research

Studied LEGO Builder and BrickLink Studio to identify gaps in creative generation tools.

Planning

Designed inventory system and AI recommendation structure.

Development

Built responsive web prototype using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

Testing

Improved suggestion logic and UI experience for usability.

Current SIP Progress

September 26, 2026 - Adaptive Challenge System Development

This week I made major progress on the BrickBrain prototype by developing the Adaptive Challenge System. Users can now generate challenges using BrickBrain's recommended difficulty or customize the difficulty and build type themselves.

After completing a challenge, users can rate it as Too Easy, Just Right, or Too Hard. BrickBrain uses this feedback to adjust its recommended difficulty. The prototype also tracks completed challenges, feedback, statistics, and recent challenge history between sessions.

My next major step is developing the Inventory Management System and connecting it to Adaptive Challenges so BrickBrain can generate challenges based on both the user's progress and the LEGO pieces they own.

Current BrickBrain prototype dashboard.

Adaptive Challenge System showing challenge generation, difficulty recommendations, statistics, and feedback.

September 13, 2026 - SIP Progress Update

Since finishing SIP 311, I have continued refining how I want BrickBrain to proceed and how I want this next stage of development to be. BrickBrain has evolved into an adaptive LEGO design coach that will use a user's LEGO interests, selected difficulty, and inventory to make personalized challenges.

My current focus is planning the inventory management system as well as the adaptive challenge generation system. These two will serve as the main components around BrickBrain, allowing the users to not only receive challenges but also manage their inventory all within one system.

BrickBrain is in its development stage and isn't complete yet. My next steps in this progress are to develop the inventory system, design the challenge generation logic, and continue to improve the user interface. Future progress that is made will be documented and put here to keep track of the early and end stages of BrickBrain.
